# WorkRecords

WorkRecords is a private workplace screen recording
and archiving tool for authorized team members.

The application uses OBS Studio for screen recording
and rclone to upload recordings to Google Drive.

Its purpose is internal work quality control
and operational review.

Screen recording is performed during working hours
with prior notice to employees. Audio is not recorded.

The application is not intended for public use.
