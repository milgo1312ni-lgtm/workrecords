# WorkRecords — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026.

## Data collection

WorkRecords records the desktop screen of authorized
employees during working hours. Audio is not recorded.

## Purpose

Recordings are used for internal quality control
and operational review.

## Google Drive access

WorkRecords uses the Google Drive API through rclone
to upload, read, verify and delete recordings created
by the application.

The application uses the drive.file OAuth scope.
It does not request full access to Google Drive.

## Data storage and retention

Recordings are stored in Google Drive accounts
authorized by the users.

The intended retention period is 72 hours.
Older recordings are scheduled for deletion.

## Data sharing

Access is limited to authorized employees
and management.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

## User rights

Users may revoke Google Drive access through
their Google Account security settings.

Users may contact the administrator to request
information about their data or its deletion.
